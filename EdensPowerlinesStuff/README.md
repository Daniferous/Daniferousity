# Eden's Powerlines Stuff

This mod adds a few custom models and props for making realistic Powerlines. While this mod contains Insulators and Wires, they do not transmit power. However, this mod was made with Immersive Engineering in mind, so this mod can be used to support Immersive Engineering Insulators (and probably Create:Powergrid, too).

**See downloads below.**

## Information
### About the Mod

Eden's Powerlines - Inspired by American, Philippine, and South Korean Powerline designs... somewhat. A continuation of the old and outdated Eden's Electrical Decoration Mod.

This is a modular system. Therefore, you can create your own powerline designs. It is constructed with Immersive Engineering in mind, and now the Create Additions mod. Therefore, it is 100% compatible with Immersive Enginnering Insulators from LV to HV. IC's Wires can probably work with this mod since it works with IE's.

However, do note that the insulators in this mod DO NOT conduct electricity. They are labelled as "Insulator Extenders" because they merely extend the insulators, like how you would see in real life.

### Features
1. Concrete Poles
2. Stackable Polearms
    * Polearms can come in any shape and size as this is a modular system
    * Essentially, you can have a powerline structure that is 200 meters tall and 150 meters long... or more perhaps.
3. Equipment Platform
    * These are made to support any form of equipment, such as the mod's own transformers or other equipment from mods such as Immersive Engineering.
4. Insulators!
    * Insulators and Cutouts can be connected with wires! However, they do not transmit power. Here are a few types of insulators:
        1. Pin Insulator
        2. Dead-End Insulator
            * Comes in both Angled and Straight varieties.
        3. Cutouts
            * Interactible! Right click to open or close the fuse.
            * You will have to shift while connecting wires to it, though.
        * While this mod does not transmit power, there could be plans for such in the future. So, I decided to separate insulators and wires into five voltage tiers:
            1. LV / Low Voltage (ex. 120, 240, 480Y/277 volts)
            2. MV L1 / Medium Voltage Level 1 (ex. 11, 12.47, 13.2 kilovolts)
            3. MV L2 / Medium Voltage Level 2 (ex. 23, 23.9, 24.9 kilovolts)
            4. MV L3 / Medium Voltage Level 3 (ex. 33, 34.5, 38.1 kilovolts)
            5. HV / High Voltage (ex. 41.6, 44, 46, 69 kilovolts)
            * Insulators can accept wires that are at or lower than its voltage tier.
    * Insulator Extenders help to extend insulators to make them seem larger and they come in a few flavors:
        1. Steel Rod Insulator Extender - A simple bare steel rod.
        2. Solid Ceramic Insulator Extender
        3. Glass Ceramic Insulator Extender
        4. Lattice Extenders - Extremely modular, you can make trees with it. (though I implemented it terribly, oops)
* Everything above this point is (mostly) Survival friendly and contain recipes. Past this point, recipes are not guaranteed. However, I would personally recommend using this mod in Creative Mode. 
5. Prop Blocks, such as Transformers, HV Switches, and even downed insulators!
6. Automatic Concrete Pole and Polearm Blocks
    * Easily create powerlines using these helper blocks.
    * Though, tools such as Axiom, WorldEdit, and Effortless Building could be use as alternates to making Concrete Poles...

### AI / Artificial Intelligence Disclosure

__Eden's Powerlines Stuff, as of the latest version, still does not use any form of Generative AI for the creation of powerline models, blocks and items.__ 

Since Version 2.0.0, Generative AI is used in assisting with the creation of the Insulator-Wire system, particularly in the method of storing wire data and rendering wires whilst being compatible with shaders. This mod is a hobby of mine that would have taken several months to years to make without assistance. Thanks to AI, I am learning so much more about how rendering works and optimizations are being made to make the rendering process more efficient.

If you wish to use an AI-less version, the last AI-less version of this mod is Eden's Powerlines Stuff Release 1.6.2 which does not feature functioning insulators and wires.
